## 🧠 About Me
I am a Data Scientist bridging the gap between complex data and real-world impact. Operating at the intersection of **machine learning, prescriptive analytics, and operations research**, I build intelligent, data-driven systems designed to support critical decision-making. 
While my research background is rooted in deep learning and advanced AI methodologies, my true passion lies in solving operational bottlenecks. I specialize in applying **causal inference** and designing agent-based systems to tackle optimization problems. For me, the goal isn't just predicting the future—it's improving efficiency, adaptability, and decision quality within strict operational constraints and under deep uncertainty.
